# Blog Writer

Use this skill when the user wants to write, rewrite, structure, or polish a blog post.

## Goal

Produce blog content that is:

- clear and useful to the target reader
- easy to scan and understand
- organized around a strong narrative arc
- persuasive without sounding promotional
- ready for editorial review and publication

## Workflow

### 1. Clarify the objective
Before writing, identify:

- the target audience
- the main problem or question to solve
- the desired outcome (educate, persuade, convert, inform)
- the expected tone (friendly, authoritative, technical, conversational)

### 2. Build a brief
Use the brief template in `references/blog-brief-template.md` to capture:

- title and angle
- audience and intent
- key takeaways
- recommended structure
- CTA or conversion goal

### 3. Draft a strong structure
For most blog posts, use this flow:

1. Hook the reader in the first 2-3 sentences
2. State the problem or opportunity clearly
3. Explain the context and why it matters
4. Present the main framework, comparison, or process
5. Add evidence, examples, and practical guidance
6. Summarize with takeaways or next steps

### 4. Write with editorial quality
Apply these rules:

- lead with value, not background clutter
- prefer short paragraphs and strong topic sentences
- keep sentences direct and readable
- avoid jargon unless the audience expects it
- use examples, lists, and headings to improve scanning
- keep the message consistent across the full post

### 5. Improve and polish
Review for:

- clarity and flow
- repetition and filler
- strong transitions between sections
- headline and subheading effectiveness
- alignment with audience intent

### 6. Final teaching output
When delivering the blog post, provide:

- the final article text or a structured draft
- a short summary of the writing choices made
- optional suggestions for title variants, CTA, and structure improvements

## Recommended article structure

- Title
- Intro with promise and context
- Main body with 3-5 sections
- Practical examples or screenshots if relevant
- Final takeaway and CTA

## Writing guidance

- Use an engaging but credible tone
- Make the reader feel understood
- Give actionable advice, not vague statements
- Avoid generic fluff; every section should support the article premise
- Favor clarity over cleverness
