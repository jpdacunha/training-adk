# Editorial Checklist

## Structure

- Does the post start with a clear promise or problem statement?
- Is the article easy to scan with headings and subheadings?
- Is the flow logical from introduction to conclusion?
- Are examples or evidence included where useful?

## Writing quality

- Is the content concise and direct?
- Are paragraphs short enough to read comfortably?
- Does the tone match the audience and subject?
- Is the vocabulary clear and natural?

## Reader value

- Does the piece answer the reader's actual question?
- Are recommendations specific and actionable?
- Does the article avoid filler or unnecessary repetition?

## Conversion and CTA

- Is there a clear next step or reason to act?
- Does the conclusion reinforce the article's value?

## Final pass

- Check grammar, punctuation, and consistency
- Remove any redundant phrases or weak transitions
- Ensure the title and intro align with the article's actual content
